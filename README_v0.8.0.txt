Ashen Builds v0.8.0 - Complete Talent Data and Stat Engine

- Includes all 27 Turtle WoW talent trees for all nine classes.
- Talent icons show current/max rank.
- Hovering a talent shows its full Turtle description.
- Left-click adds a rank; right-click removes a rank.
- Enforces level budget, row requirements, and prerequisites.
- Applies measurable character-sheet talent effects to build totals.
- Crit, hit, spell hit, dodge, parry, block, primary-stat percentages,
  armor percentages, health/mana percentages, spell/healing conversions,
  feral modifiers, and weapon skill are supported.
- Ability-only, proc-only, cooldown, cost, duration, and target-dependent
  effects remain visible in tooltips but are not incorrectly added to totals.

Talent data source: Talented-turtle v3.1.3 generated ClassData.
